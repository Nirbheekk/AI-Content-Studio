# User Guide (`USER_GUIDE.md`)

## Getting Started
1. Sign up at `/register` to create your personal studio workspace.
2. Create a Project on `/projects` or `/dashboard`.
3. Upload media on `/library` (supports MP4, MOV, WebM, PNG, JPEG, WebP, MP3, WAV, PDF).

## Creating & Editing Videos
- Use `/studio` to describe your video in plain English and generate a complete script, storyboard, composition, and rendered MP4.
- Use `/editor` for multi-track timeline editing, scene reordering, AI single-scene rewrites, Brand Kit application, undo/redo, and team comments.
- Use `/repurpose` to convert a long-form video into Reels, Shorts, LinkedIn posts, and quote graphics.

## Publishing & Analytics
- Connect accounts under `/publish`, compose platform-validated posts, and manage schedules visually in `/calendar` (Monthly/Weekly/Daily + timezone selector).
- Track real performance snapshots, usage quotas, and estimated billing under `/analytics`.
- Manage workspace members and roles (`OWNER`, `ADMIN`, `EDITOR`, `VIEWER`) under `/settings`.
