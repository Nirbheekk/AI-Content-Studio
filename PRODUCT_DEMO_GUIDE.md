# Product Demo Guide (`PRODUCT_DEMO_GUIDE.md`)

1. **Onboard (`/register`)**: Create an account + workspace; auto-provisions default Brand Kit and redirects to `/dashboard`.
2. **Brand Identity (`/brand-kit`)**: Configure brand colors, typography, caption style, and tone of voice.
3. **Upload Assets (`/library`)**: Upload raw video/image/audio files (magic-byte verified and stored in MinIO).
4. **AI Production (`/studio`)**: Select project + platform (`9:16` Instagram Reel / TikTok / YouTube Short), enter natural-language goal, and click **Produce & Render**. Watch live SSE stages, play the rendered MP4 in-browser, review Visual QA, request conversational revisions, and click **Approve**.
5. **Fine-Tune (`/editor` & `/repurpose`)**: Scrub frames, reorder/edit scenes, switch aspect ratios (`9:16`, `16:9`, `1:1`, `4:5`), export `.srt`/`.vtt` subtitles, compare versions, and repurpose long-form assets into multi-platform campaigns.
6. **Schedule & Publish (`/publish` & `/calendar`)**: Connect social account, validate platform rules, schedule or publish approved content, and drag-and-drop reschedule on the Content Calendar.
7. **Measure (`/analytics`)**: Inspect post performance, generate evidence-based vs AI insights, monitor quotas, and export CSV reports.
